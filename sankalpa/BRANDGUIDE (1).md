# Kodagu Sankalpa — Brand & Identity Guide

*An initiative of Kodagu.ai · Version 1.0 — August 2026*

**Kodagu Sankalpa** (ಸಂಕಲ್ಪ, "resolve") is Kodagu.ai's quarterly innovation challenge — a ₹1 lakh prize to find and incubate local solutions to Kodagu's problems. The identity is **coffee-forward**: it wears Kodagu's defining crop with pride, and it should feel **bold, grounded, and determined** — the visual equivalent of *sankalpa* itself.

---

## 1. Logo

The logo is a stacked wordmark — **KODAGU** in espresso black over **SANKALPA** in Kodagu gold — with two coffee beans nestled at the top-right and the line **INNOVATION CHALLENGE** ruled beneath. Together they say exactly what this is, and where it's from.

**Primary logo (full colour)** — use wherever there's room: website header, posters, title slides, certificates, merchandise.

**Coffee-bean icon** — the two beans alone. Use for the app icon, favicon, social avatar, stamps, and any small or square space.

## 2. Colour

Pulled directly from the mark — the black of roasted intent, the gold of Kodagu's harvest, the brown of the bean, on warm cream.

### Core palette
| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Espresso Black** | `#171717` | 23, 23, 23 | Primary text, "KODAGU", headings |
| **Kodagu Gold** | `#A97C24` | 169, 124, 36 | The brand colour — "SANKALPA", buttons, accents, links |
| **Coffee Brown** | `#4E351B` | 78, 53, 27 | Beans, depth, secondary accents, borders |
| **Cream** | `#FBF7EF` | 251, 247, 239 | Warm background (softer than white) |

### Supporting neutrals & tints
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#211B12` | Body text (warm near-black) |
| **Sand** | `#EFE7D6` | Cards, dividers, muted surfaces |
| **Gold tint** | `#E7CE97` | Highlights, wash fills, hover |
| **Gold dark** | `#7E5A16` | Pressed states, gold text on cream (better contrast) |

### Accessibility
Espresso Black and Ink on Cream both pass WCAG AA for body text. **Kodagu Gold (`#A97C24`) is borderline for small text on cream** — use it for headings, large text, buttons (with dark text), icons and accents; for small gold-on-cream text use **Gold dark `#7E5A16`**. White text is legible on Espresso Black, Coffee Brown, and Gold dark — not on Kodagu Gold at small sizes.

## 3. Typography

The wordmark is a **tall, bold, condensed grotesque** in all-caps — strong and confident. Match it:

- **Display / headings:** **Oswald** or **Anton** (bold condensed, Google Fonts) in UPPERCASE — echoes the logo. Use for section titles and big numbers.
- **Body / UI:** **Inter** or **Source Sans 3** — clean and legible, including small sizes and tables (use tabular figures for the prize amounts).
- **Kannada (essential):** **Noto Sans Kannada** — for ಸಂಕಲ್ಪ and all Kannada copy; test key screens in Kannada.
- **Accent / eyebrow:** condensed caps, letter-spaced (~0.15em), like "INNOVATION CHALLENGE" in the logo.

---

## 4. The coffee-bean motif

The two beans are the signature graphic device. Use them (subtly) as: bullet markers, section dividers, a loading state, a watermark on certificates, or a repeating low-opacity background texture. Keep them Coffee Brown or Gold; don't stretch or recolour them outside the palette.

---

## 5. Do & Don't

**Do:** keep the stacked KODAGU/SANKALPA proportion; give the logo clear space equal to the height of the beans; use gold as the hero accent; pair with cream and real Kodagu coffee/landscape photography; use the bean icon for small spaces.

**Don't:** recolour outside the palette; add gradients, shadows, or outlines; stretch or rotate; set the wordmark in a different font; put the full-colour logo on a busy or clashing background (use the white version on dark).

---

## 6. Relationship to the Kodagu.ai family

Kodagu.ai's initiatives each have their own accent while sharing a civic, land-rooted spirit: **Aane Alert** (forest green, elephant), **Nela** (green/soil/gold, land-health), and **Kodagu Sankalpa** (espresso/gold/coffee, the innovation prize). Sankalpa is deliberately the warmest and boldest — it's the rallying, human-facing front door of the whole initiative. When co-branding, present "an initiative of Kodagu.ai" in small caps beneath the Sankalpa logo.

---

## 7. Voice

Determined, warm, plain-spoken, proud of Kodagu. It speaks to a planter, a student, a village maker — not an investor. Short sentences, concrete nouns, Kannada-first. It rallies ("Kodagu's next solution could be yours") without hype.
