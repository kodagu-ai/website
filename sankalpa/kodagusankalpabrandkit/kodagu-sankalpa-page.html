What is Kodagu Sankalpa

A prize that turns Kodagu's ideas into working solutions

Kodagu is full of people who know its problems best — planters, students, panchayat members, families at the forest's edge. Kodagu Sankalpa is where their ideas get backed. Four times a year we pick one Kodagu problem, invite solutions from the people who live it, and fund the best one from prototype to a real pilot on the ground. It is the innovation engine of Kodagu.ai — alongside open tools like Aane Alert and Nela.

How it works

One challenge a quarter · ~90 days to pilot

A simple, published rhythm so anyone can plan for the theme that fits them.

01 · WEEKS 1–4

Open & scout

We announce the quarter's theme and go find ideas — meetups, village scouting, and entries by form, WhatsApp voice note, or in person, in Kannada, Kodava thakk, or English.

02 · WEEKS 5–8

Shortlist & mentor

The strongest entries are shortlisted, everyone gets honest feedback, and a short bootcamp sharpens ideas into something buildable.

03 · WEEKS 9–12

Judge & award

A local panel plus one theme expert picks the winner against a published rubric. The winner gets the first prize tranche, a mentor, and a pilot site.

04 · ONGOING

Build & prove

Prize money is released as the solution hits real milestones — and we help winners apply for follow-on funding worth far more.

How we judge

Five things we look for

The rubric is public from day one. Every entrant is scored the same way — and gets written feedback.

1

Impact for Kodagu

Does it solve a real, felt Kodagu problem for real people here?

2

Feasibility

Can it be built & piloted with ₹1 lakh + mentorship in one season?

3

Community-rooted

Is it designed with the people affected — do they want it?

4

Team & grit

Can you execute and stick with it through the pilot?

5

Path forward

Could it outlive the prize — via revenue, a partner, or funding?
