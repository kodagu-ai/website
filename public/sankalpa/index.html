What is Kodagu Sankalpa

A prize that turns Kodagu's ideas into working solutions

Kodagu is full of people who know its problems best — planters, students, panchayat members, families at the forest's edge. Sankalpa is where their ideas get backed: we invite solutions from the people who live the problem, and fund the best one from prototype to a real pilot on the ground. It is the innovation engine of Kodagu.ai — alongside open tools like Aane Alert.

How it works

From idea to a real pilot — in one season

A simple, published rhythm so anyone can plan. Enter in Kannada, Kodava thakk, or English — writing, a voice note, or in person all count.

01 · AUG–OCT

Open & scout

We announce the open challenge and go find ideas — meetups, village scouting, and entries by form, WhatsApp, or in person.

02 · NOVEMBER

Shortlist & mentor

The strongest entries are shortlisted, everyone gets honest feedback, and a short bootcamp sharpens ideas into something buildable.

03 · END NOV

Judge & award

A local panel plus one expert picks the winner against a public rubric. The winner gets the first prize tranche, a mentor and a pilot site.

04 · DEC ONWARD

Build & prove

Prize money is released as the solution hits real milestones — and we help winners apply for follow-on funding worth far more.

How we judge

Five things we look for

The rubric is public from day one. Every entrant is scored the same way — and gets written feedback.

1

Impact for Kodagu

Does it solve a real, felt Kodagu problem for real people here?

2

Feasibility

Can it be built & piloted with ₹1 lakh + mentorship in one season?

3

Community-rooted

Is it designed with the people affected — do they want it?

4

Team & grit

Can you execute and stick with it through the pilot?

5

Path forward

Could it outlive the prize — via revenue, a partner, or funding?
